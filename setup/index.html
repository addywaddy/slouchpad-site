Set up Slouchpad

Slouchpad needs its free companion app, Slouchpad for Mac, on your Mac. Three steps: put it on your Mac, let it control the pointer and keys, then scan its QR code with your iPhone.

DownloadSlouchpad for Mac

Using Slouchpad 1.1 or earlier, with a PIN? See the 1.1 guide.

Open this page on your Mac. macOS 13 or later. Don't have the iPhone app yet? Get it from the App Store.

  1. Install Slouchpad for Mac

    Download Slouchpad for Mac. Open Slouchpad.dmg from your Downloads folder, drag the Slouchpad icon onto the Applications folder in the window that opens, then open Slouchpad from Applications. If you open it from somewhere else, it offers to move itself to Applications. It has no window of its own: it lives in your menu bar, behind a couch icon.

  2. Permit

    On your Mac: Slouchpad needs the Accessibility permission to move the pointer and press keys for your phone. Click the couch icon in the menu bar: if a One more step card is showing, click its button to open System Settings. Under Privacy & Security, then Accessibility, switch Slouchpad on. (On the newest macOS this page may be called Device Control and Data Access.) The card goes away by itself once it is done.

    On your iPhone: When Slouchpad asks to find devices on your local network, tap Allow, so it can reach your Mac. If you tapped Don’t Allow, open the iPhone’s Settings, choose Slouchpad and switch Local Network on.

  3. Connect

    Click the couch icon in your Mac’s menu bar. The menu shows a QR code under Pair your iPhone. On your iPhone, open Slouchpad, tap Scan QR Code and point the camera at the code (the first time, allow camera access). The menu on your Mac then says Connected, and the iPhone shows its controls.

    You can also scan the code with the iPhone’s Camera app. If you can’t scan, click Copy link instead in the Mac’s menu, send the link to your iPhone in a message and open it there.

    While the app is open, your iPhone reconnects to your Mac by itself. When you close the app it forgets your Mac, so you scan the code again next time. If you scan a new code, for example after reinstalling Slouchpad for Mac, it replaces the old pairing.

Your iPhone and Mac work best on the same Wi-Fi network. If the Slouchpad menu on your Mac says Over the direct Wi-Fi link, the pointer may be less smooth; join both devices to the same network. Brightness works on the screens macOS lets apps control, such as the built-in display and Apple displays. Many other monitors can only be adjusted with their own buttons.

Can’t find your Mac, or something else not working? See Support, or write to support@slouchpad.com.
